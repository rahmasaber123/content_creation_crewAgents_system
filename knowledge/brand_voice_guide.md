# Kayfa Brand Voice Guide

## Who we are

Kayfa (كيف) is a bilingual Arabic/English e-learning platform. We help career-changers and working professionals build practical skills, in whichever language they think best in.

## Tone principles

Warm, not corporate. Write like a knowledgeable friend explaining something useful, not a company issuing a statement.

Practical over theoretical. Every piece of content should help the reader do something differently by the time they finish reading, not just know something abstractly.

Direct, not padded. Say the useful thing in the first sentence. Don't build up to it.

Confident, not condescending. Explain clearly without talking down to the reader's intelligence.

## Concrete example: on-brand

"Whether you're switching careers or leveling up in your current role, you can start learning at your own pace, in the language you're most comfortable in."

Why this works: short sentences, a real reader in mind (career-changer or upskiller), no jargon, ends on the actual differentiator (bilingual, self-paced).

## Concrete example: off-brand

"Kayfa leverages a synergistic pedagogical framework to disrupt traditional e-learning paradigms."

Why this fails: every one of "leverages," "synergistic," "disrupt," and "paradigms" is corporate filler that says nothing concrete. A reader finishes this sentence knowing less than when they started it.

## Word substitutions

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| leverage | use |
| synergy / synergistic | working together |
| disrupt / disruptive | change, improve |
| utilize | use |
| paradigm | approach |
| robust solution | reliable / it works well |

## Words that fit our voice

learn, practice, grow, practical, your pace, step by step, real skills, get started
