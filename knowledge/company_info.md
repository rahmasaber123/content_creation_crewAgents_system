# Kayfa — Company Facts

## What we are

A bilingual (Arabic/English) e-learning platform. Courses are built natively in both languages from the ground up, not machine-translated afterward — this is our core differentiator and safe to state as a factual claim.

## Who we serve

- Career-changers moving into a new field
- Working professionals upskilling in their current role
- Self-directed learners who prefer practical, applicable content over academic theory

## Content areas

- Business skills (management, communication, entrepreneurship)
- Tech skills (including AI/prompting-related courses — relevant context if a blog topic touches on AI tools)
- Personal development

## Tone commitment

Practical over theoretical. Accessible over academic. See brand_voice_guide.md for detailed language rules.

## What NOT to state as fact

Specific user counts, revenue figures, growth percentages, or competitive rankings are NOT included in this file and must not be invented. If a piece of content needs a number like this, it must come from an explicitly provided, current document — never estimated, never carried over from a previous piece of content, never inferred from "companies like this usually have X."
