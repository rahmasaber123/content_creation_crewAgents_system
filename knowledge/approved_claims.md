# Approved Claims — Kayfa

Use these exact claims when writing about Kayfa itself. Do not paraphrase them into something stronger or more specific than what's written here.

## Approved

- "Kayfa offers courses in both Arabic and English, built natively for each language."
- "Kayfa focuses on practical, applicable skills over academic theory."
- "Kayfa serves career-changers and working professionals."

## Forbidden without an explicit, current source document

- Any specific user/learner count ("10,000+ learners," "thousands of students")
- Any revenue, funding, or valuation figure
- Any growth percentage or "fastest-growing" claim
- Any comparison naming a specific competitor by name
- Any claim about course completion rates or outcomes without a cited study

## Rule for the Writer-Editor and Accuracy checks

If a draft contains a number or claim about Kayfa that isn't in the "Approved" list above, and wasn't explicitly provided in the task input, treat it as unverified — either remove it or flag it in the accuracy notes rather than publishing it as fact.
